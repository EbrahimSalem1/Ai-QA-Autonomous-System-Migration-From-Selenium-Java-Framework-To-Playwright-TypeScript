## Story
**As a** new visitor of the OpenCart store **I want to** create a customer account **so that** I can log in, save a wish list and check out faster.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1 — Successful registration:** valid first/last name, unique e-mail, strong password, Privacy Policy accepted → heading **Your Account Has Been Created!**
- **AC2 — Duplicate e-mail is rejected:** registering an existing e-mail → **Warning: E-Mail Address is already registered!**
- **AC3 — Privacy Policy must be accepted:** submit without accepting → Privacy Policy warning, no account created.
- **AC4 — Mandatory fields are validated:** empty submit → field errors for First Name, Last Name, E-Mail, Password.

**Out of scope:** newsletter, customer groups, custom fields, confirmation e-mail.
