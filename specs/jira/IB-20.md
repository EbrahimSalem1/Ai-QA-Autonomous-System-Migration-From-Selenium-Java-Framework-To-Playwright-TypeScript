## Story
**As a** shopper **I want to** check out the products in my cart **so that** I can place an order.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1 — Guest checkout:** Guest Checkout, details + address, **Flat Shipping Rate**, agree to Terms, **Cash On Delivery**, **Confirm Order** → **Your order has been placed!**
- **AC2 — Logged in:** with MacBook in cart → **Checkout** opens without guest/register options.
- **AC3 — Empty cart:** open checkout → Shopping Cart page with **Your shopping cart is empty!**
- **AC4 — Terms required** *(updated after automation)*: terms not accepted → **Confirm Order** disabled; accept → enabled.

**Out of scope:** real payment gateways, coupons, registering during checkout.
