## Story
**As a** registered customer **I want to** log in and out of my account **so that** I can access my account features securely.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1 — Valid credentials:** e-mail + password → **My Account** page.
- **AC2 — Wrong password:** → **Warning: No match for E-Mail Address and/or Password.**
- **AC3 — Unknown e-mail:** → **Warning: No match for E-Mail Address and/or Password.**
- **AC4 — Logout:** My Account menu → Logout → **Account Logout** page.

**Out of scope:** forgotten-password e-mail, lockout after repeated failures.
