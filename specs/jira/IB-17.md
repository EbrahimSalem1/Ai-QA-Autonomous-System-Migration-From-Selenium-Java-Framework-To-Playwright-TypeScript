## Story
**As a** shopper **I want to** review and adjust the products in my cart **so that** I buy exactly what I need.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1 — Added product appears:** iPhone added → cart lists **iPhone** with quantity **1**.
- **AC2 — Update quantity:** quantity **3** + Update → **Success: You have modified your shopping cart!**, quantity 3.
- **AC3 — Remove:** remove the only product → **Your shopping cart is empty!**
- **AC4 — Empty cart:** nothing added → **Your shopping cart is empty!**

**Out of scope:** coupons, gift certificates, shipping/tax estimates. Cart scenarios run as guest (parallel-safe).
